Handover from the other thread (9939e89c, orch-agent/): 3 corrections for your new "3 sites, then ERP" plan, based on our measured runs.

1. ERP helpers in the SAME browser, in their own tab, not new logins in other browsers.
   - Measured: an ERP owner in tab 1 plus a helper in tab 2 of the same Chrome (each with its own Playwright MCP server attached via --cdp-endpoint to that Chrome) worked twice (the ERP tab experiment and the E2E run). The helper inherited login + passed gates and was on its first search 7 s after taking a group; in separate browsers, helpers needed 72-92 s.
   - The ERP owner's own note: "don't open the login in two tabs: 403". Parallel logins are risky, the shared session isn't.
   - Code: orchestrated/erp_tabs_test.py (experiment), orch-agent/main.py research() (two servers per Chrome: b{n}o/b{n}h).

2. A form scout sees only part of the form, and sessions expire quickly.
   - The wizard only saves with "Weiter"; the configurator (step 3+) only appears after steps 1-2 are filled in, so a scout that "only looks" never gets there.
   - Measured: at the start of our submission (~5 min after research began), BOTH the tender portal and the webmail showed the login page again. The research sessions had expired. In the first E2E submission, the portal session also expired at the final confirmation (~4.5 min into the form) and everything was lost.
   - What actually helped: (a) material attributes per position in the bid text, so there's no research during the form; (b) a prompt rule "look at a step once, then fill all its fields in ONE action, no pauses". Result: 1 attempt, 49 instead of 106 tool calls, $1.15 instead of $2.26, score 100. The remaining time (~135 s of 352 s) was Opus writing configurator scripts, which a scout would hardly change.

3. Reuse the submission parts from orch-agent/ instead of rebuilding them:
   - resolver.py: Resolution.details = matched material plus derived attributes per position (bore, nominal stroke, pressure, mounting, certification, catalog_group); also in bid_engine/, with a test.
   - main.py: bid_text(answer, omitted, details) with "material details" per position; the SUBMIT prompt with the fast-form rules and truthful gate context (GATES, no site quotes); submit() gets TWO already-logged-in browsers (tender + mail) after fresh_tabs().
   - evidence.py: Num/Count number parsing ({"raw","format"} for ambiguous formats like "12.345" de) plus the STORED context for reading saved evidence back; the store rejects unknown field names (in your case the check would be useful when merging the JSON parts).
   - Still open there: the contact email (vertrieb@<domain>) in the bid text (saves a mail login, ~20 s), and testing Sonnet for the submission with the gate context.
